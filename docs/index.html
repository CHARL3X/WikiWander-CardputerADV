Wikiwander.

a small pocket library for the M5Stack Cardputer ADV

Wikipedia's entire pool of articles, distilled into 240 by 135 pixels of warm sepia text. Pull a random page from the curated lead-summary feed, see what happened on this date in history, search by name, save to a tiny library on the SD card, walk through tappable links one after another.

Wikiwander reads like a small book carried in a pocket. The display is unbuffered, so every frame is composed in RAM and pushed once. The body text is full of the same inline references the encyclopedia carries on the web — press Tab and the next one lights up; press ⏎ to follow it. The walk has a trail; press b to go back.

Colophon Body set in EB Garamond, display in Fraunces at maximum optical-size axis. The device's on-screen text is rendered in M5Stack's FreeSerifBoldItalic for titles, Font2 bitmap for body — Editorial italic on a 240-pixel screen, the smallest book ever printed.

§ I

What it does.

r ∽

Random.

Pulls a curated summary from Wikipedia's random pool. A built-in filter quietly re-rolls past biographies — the encyclopedia is  30% people if you let it be.

t ⏚

Today.

What happened on this date. A scrollable list of curated events, each with a year and a primary article you can open and walk from.

/ ⌕

Search.

Type to query MediaWiki's opensearch. Live results below the input — Tab to focus the list, arrows or 1–9 to pick.

Tab ∝

Inline links.

The lead-section HTML is fetched separately, tokenized into text and link runs, rendered with per-segment color. Tab cycles each link in turn; ⏎ follows.

b ↶

Walk trail.

Every forward jump pushes onto an in-memory breadcrumb. Step back through your walk one article at a time — a small chevron in the corner shows how deep you are.

s ❧

Library.

Save articles to /Wikiwander/articles/ as markdown with a frontmatter header. The HTML sidecar preserves the links so saved articles stay tappable.

q ▦

QR share.

Generate a code of the article's canonical URL. Scan it with a phone to continue reading on something larger, or to send a friend down the rabbit hole.

u ↻

Resume.

The device remembers your last article. Battery dies mid-walk, you power on, press u, you're right where you left off.

c ¶

Settings.

Text size in three steps, people filter, audio mute. The body font scales between Font2 bitmap and FreeSans 9/12pt vector — pick what your eyes prefer.

❦
§ II — the walk

One article links to the next.

Open any article, press ⏎ at the end, and three forks open: wander from this, random, or today's events. The contextual path uses MediaWiki's morelike: search to surface six semantically related pages.

Every step pushes the previous article onto a trail. From any point you can go forward (random / related / link), back (one article at a time), or up to home. The trail caps at twelve entries — enough to recover from a wrong turn, small enough that fifty articles into a session your heap is still calm.

§ IV — how to flash

Pocket-sized.

Wikiwander runs as a guest app under bmorcelli's Launcher, so the install is two files on an SD card and one menu pick. WiFi credentials live in a shared wifi.txt at the SD root — no setup app required.

  1. Flash bmorcelli's Launcher to the Cardputer ADV using the web flasher. One-time, ~30 seconds.
  2. Drop Wikiwander.bin into /apps/ on the SD card.
  3. Create /Cardputer/wifi.txt: SSID and password on alternating lines.
  4. Boot the launcher, choose SD › Wikiwander.bin, install. Done.
